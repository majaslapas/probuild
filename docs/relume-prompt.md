# Relume prompts

Angliski, jo Relume angļu promptus saprot precīzāk. Ja vajag latviešu tekstus wireframe'os, pievieno rindu `Write all copy in Latvian.`

Nomas cena vēl nav izlemta (konkrēta cena vai "pēc vienošanās"), tāpēc promptā ir "price".

```text
Company: ProBuildGroup (probuildgroup.lv) is a construction and renovation company in Latvia (Salaspils area) that also rents out construction equipment and tools. The website's goal is to generate inquiries for construction services and equipment rental. It will be built in Webflow.

Create a 6-page website plus one CMS template page. Include ONLY the pages and sections listed below. Do not add a blog, FAQ, pricing page, careers, newsletter, testimonials page or any other pages or sections.

GLOBAL
- Navbar with links to all 6 pages
- Chatbot widget placeholder, available on every page

1. HOME
- Hero: company name, short tagline covering the main services (construction, renovation, equipment rental), CTA buttons ("Learn more" / "Get information") leading to Services and Equipment Rental, background image of real completed work
- Main services: short overview of the key services, linking to the Services page
- Reviews: embedded Google Business reviews

2. SERVICES
- Service cards: each has a title, a short description and a link to more info or to the calculator section. Examples: facades (thermowood, charred wood boards, tongue-and-groove cladding), property maintenance contracts
- Price calculator: compact, intuitive interactive estimator. The user selects the work types they need, enters the quantity or area, and sees an approximate total. It must NOT look like a large spreadsheet
- Reviews: embedded Google Business reviews

3. EQUIPMENT RENTAL (CMS collection, the owner adds and edits items)
- Rental item cards (e.g. tractor with front loader, rebar tools): image, short description, price, "Send request" button
- Request form: no online payment. The request goes to email/CRM and the final price is agreed after contact
- Layout must allow grouping items into categories if more product groups are added later

4. PORTFOLIO (CMS collection, the owner adds projects; this is the main showcase)
- Filter by work type (e.g. electrical installation, foundations, timber frame)
- Project cards: before/after image plus quick tags visible without opening the project (work types done, area in m², price per m²)

5. PORTFOLIO PROJECT (CMS template page)
- Before/after photo gallery and project details (work types, m², price per m²)

6. ABOUT US
- Company story, history and experience
- Team
- Partners, brands and tools the company works with (logo strip, builds trust)

7. CONTACT
- Contact form (submissions go to the CRM)
- Phone and email
- Location: Salaspils area
- Links to social media and the Google Business profile
```
